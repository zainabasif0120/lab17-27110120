How it works

Three quick steps, then everyone knows their share. The maths is less awkward than deciding who got the last naan.

  1. Enter the bill

    Type in the bill amount in rupees.

  2. Choose your group

    Set how many people are sharing the meal.

  3. Add tip and tax

    Pick a tip and enter the tax percentage. Your per-person amount updates as you type.

One worked example

A Rs 4,000 bill split between 4 people, with a 10% tip and 16% tax:
